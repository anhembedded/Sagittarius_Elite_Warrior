### Decisions in stage 1
- **Emergency stop stops every enabled venue.** It is on every mode's toolbar (HLD §11.2.2), where no venue shows; the person pressing it wants trading stopped. Its confirmation names each venue and what it closes there ("Stop everything", Cancel the default). Each venue's stop keeps its own ownership and fencing (`DeskSessionControls`).
- **Only enabled venues are listed**, read once at boot (`TradingModule.boot` stashes `IVenueContexts.enabled()`); enabling one still needs a restart, as before. With none, the mode says where to turn one on and holds nothing that sends; its commands stay disabled.
- **Window → Reset layout resets the venue shown.** Each venue keeps its own layout, so the other's is left as the person left it. It also avoids a Qt quirk: a hidden `QMainWindow`'s restored state is laid out only once it shows, and the tab bars of the state it replaced stay drawn over the panels (measured 2026-10-06). For the same reason the conformance checks measure the surface a mode shows; the Trade mode is measured with each venue shown in turn.
- **One tabbed side per page.** Tabbed panels on two sides, made before the window first shows, leave a stale tab bar drawn over the panels (the same quirk). So Order entry stands above Account summary, both in view (the entry scrolls, once, at its panel), and the bottom is the tabbed side; Strategy was tabbed there until it left in stage 4.
- **The right column runs the page's full height** (review of PR #375). Beside the chart alone, the bottom panels' hints left Order entry about 70 px at 1366×768, so New order… focused a price field scrolled out of view. The right dock area now holds the bottom-right corner, and every bottom panel asks for its minimum (Strategy too). Measured after the change: Order entry 330 px at 1024×700 and 1366×768, and the price field in view at all three sizes (`test_new_order_focuses_a_field_the_person_sees`). The price paid: at 1024×700 the bottom tab titles are elided (each tab's tooltip and View still name the panel), and at 1366×768 and below the Futures Buy/Sell buttons sit about 55 px below the panel's fold; Tab from the price field scrolls an enabled button into view. Spot shows them at every size.
- **The Strategy panel stays until `EPIC-033K` stage 3** moves arming to the Bots mode; deleting it first would take arming away from the app. It is not one of HLD §11.2.1's panels, so the first criterion stays open until then.
- **The symbol is on the page's header toolbar**, not a context bar: a context bar is a second row under an empty first one.
- **The conformance suite boots with no venue enabled**, so it measures the Trade mode's notice alone. `test_trade_mode_conformance.py` measures the mode with both venues built against the fake Binance server, at each window size with each venue shown, and pictures each; its known failures are ratcheted in `baseline_workbench_conformance.json` under `failing_with_venues`.

- **Desktop E2E harness (2026-10-06).** `scripts/workbench_desktop_e2e.py` runs the journey on a real display: two real boots of the app over a scratch `SEW_DATA_ROOT`, a real mouse click on each mode's button, every mode rearranged, the app closed through its real shutdown and started again, each dock and toolbar compared, a picture of each step. Its first run, on an X server (Xvfb, not offscreen) in the cloud session, found `BUG-154` (the environment banner could be hidden from the toolbar menu), fixed in the same pull request; after the fix it reads `RESULT: PASS` for all five modes. What stays open is the same command on the owner's Windows display: `python scripts/workbench_desktop_e2e.py`, then a look at the pictures it names.

## Implementation notes (written when done)
**Stage 1 (2026-10-06).** `ui/trade/`: `trade_screen.py` (route `trade`, the default mode, the `dollar-sign` icon), `trade_commands.py` (Qt-free: the Venue choices, Enable live trading, New order… and Emergency stop with `mode=None`, and View → Chart for the chosen venue's chart), `venue_choice.py` (`VenueChoice`, the pattern of `MarketChoice`), `trade_command_binding.py` (the commands to the chosen venue's desk; the chart mirror follows it), `trade_view.py` (`TradeView`, an `ISurfaceStack`), `trade_presenter.py`, `trade_dependencies.py`, `preview.py`. `DeskView` is a venue's page on a `WorkbenchSurface`, no `PageShell`; `DeskPresenter` is a plain presenter whose `request_toggle`, `request_emergency_stop`, `request_new_order` and `commandStateChanged` the mode binds, and it refuses another venue's strategy as it refused another venue's ports (`BOT-158`). The venues' lines share the mode's one Output channel, each prefixed with its venue. `support/ui_kit/surface_stack.py` (`ISurfaceStack`) and `ModeHost` let a mode show one of several surfaces; `support/ui_kit/minimum_hint_slot.py` keeps the bottom panels at their minimum so the chart keeps the larger share. Deleted: `futures_desk_screen.py`, `spot_desk_screen.py`, `desk_factories.py`, `desk_commands.py`, `disabled_desk_presenter.py`. Measured: the conformance baseline's `trading.futures` and `trading.spot` rows (`no_style_sheet`, the `PageShell` header) are gone and `failing` is empty; with both venues built the mode passes every check at 1024×700, 1366×768 and 1920×1080 but `toolbar_actions_in_a_menu` (the tables' Cancel order, Cancel all and Close at market, stage 3); the ruff baseline loses `desk_factories.py`'s 7 function-local imports. The criterion "The user confirms one order round-trip per venue on Testnet" is the user's.

**Stage 2 (2026-10-06).** Each account table is a bottom panel of its own, in HLD §11.2.1's order, tabbed with Equity (and Strategy until stage 4): Positions on Futures or Assets on Spot (in front), Open orders, Order history, Trade history. `AccountTabsPanel` keeps what the tables share and lays nothing out (`panels()`); it owns the one table a venue does not show, hidden. Hide other pairs is View → Hide other pairs, a checkable command following the venue chosen (`HIDE_OTHER_PAIRS`; its access key is `s`, every other letter of it being another View item's); the outcome of a cancel or a close, and a blocked strategy order, is said on the venue's status line (`messageShown`). The panels keep their minimum height (`MinimumHintSlot`), and the summary too, so the chart and the entry keep the room. Measured: the tables' own toolbars (Cancel order, Cancel all…, Close at market…) are still the mode's one ratcheted finding, which stage 3 closes. Seen in the pictures: at 1366×768 the window's Output pane, shared by every mode, takes about 200 px of height, so the Order entry scrolls; that is the shell's, not this mode's.

**Stage 3 (2026-10-06).** Trade → Cancel order (the platform's Delete), Cancel all orders (also on the toolbar) and Close position drive the chosen venue's account tables' own actions (`AccountTabsPanel.menu_actions()`, followed by the ui kit's `ActionMirror`, which the chart and the Bots kinds use too), which ask first and act on what the table selects or shows; Close position is off on Spot. The tables have no toolbar; their rows' context menus repeat the commands, and the panel's Cancel order no longer holds Del, which the command owns. Every question of the mode asks with its verbs, the safe one the default and Esc keeping it (`support/ui_kit/verb_confirmation.py`): Place order / Cancel, Cancel order / Keep order, Cancel all orders / Keep orders, Close position / Keep position, and Enable trading / Keep it off when Enable live trading turns trading on (never when it turns it off); the Engine's action would ask on every trigger, so the mode asks itself (`TradeView.ask_to_enable`). Measured: `failing_with_venues` is empty; the mode passes every check at every size with both venues built.

What is left, and where it goes: the Strategy panel, not one of HLD §11.2.1's, leaves with arming (`EPIC-033K` stage 3, stage 4 here), which closes the first criterion; the desktop E2E (open, use, rearrange, restart) and the user's Testnet round trip per venue, which no session can run. Seen in the pictures and not this mode's: the window's Output pane takes about 200 px of a 768 px window in every mode.
