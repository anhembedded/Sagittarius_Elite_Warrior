## 1. Context

**The problem.** A Futures bot that halts (`price_feed_stale`, stream down, a rejected key, a switch-off) closes its position, or, for the halts that cannot send an order, leaves it bare. The only protection that survives a dead app is an order resting on the exchange. `EPIC-039H` closes on every halt and says so (`bots/application/services/positions/closer_futures.py` → `PositionExposureCloser.closes_on_halt`), so today nothing is left bare by design, and nothing recovers by itself either. `EPIC-039L` makes *leaving* a position acceptable by putting the exit on the exchange.

**What is established in the repository** (✅):
- `EPIC-026K` has no code: its status is Backlog and `OrderType.STOP_MARKET` is the only trace of it. Its design is a *strategy* entry flow (a stop after each entry fill, `quantity`-sized, journaled by `EPIC-026G`, exempt from the strategy limits). There is no "026K mapping" to reuse.
- What *is* there is the Algo Order mapping of `EPIC-028R`/`028I`: `trading/adapters/binance/futures_algo_order_mapper.py` → `map_order_to_futures_algo_params`, `map_futures_algo_payload_to_order`, `trading/adapters/binance/algo_update_parser.py` → `parse_algo_update`, `trading/adapters/binance/algo_order_links.py` → `AlgoOrderLinks`, and `trading/adapters/binance/futures_trading_client.py` → `FuturesTradingClient` (`get_open_orders` lists regular and algo orders together; `cancel_order` falls back to the algo cancel; `cancel_all_orders` cancels both kinds). It sends `quantity` and `reduceOnly`, `workingType=CONTRACT_PRICE` always, and never `closePosition`; `trading/contracts/order.py` → `Order` has no `close_position` field.
- The fake has the endpoint but not the semantics: `tests/sanity/fake_exchange/futures_algo_orders.py` → `FuturesAlgoOrders` stores `quantity`, ignores `closePosition`, triggers by `place_order` with that `quantity`, has no `EXPIRED`.
- A bot's orders carry `SEW-{tag}-{hex10}` (`trading/contracts/client_order_id.py` → `generate_client_order_id`, `tag_of`). `bots/application/kinds/grid/grid_housekeeping.py` → `GridHousekeeping.cancel_tagged` cancels every open order carrying the tag, so a tagged stop would be cancelled by every ordinary stop and halt unless it is excluded.
- `trading/application/session/emergency_stop/handler.py` → `EmergencyStopCommandHandler._stop` cancels every order of every symbol first (including algo orders, through `cancel_all_orders`), then closes the positions. A failed close after the cancels leaves a bare position.
- Futures mainnet is closed by `bots/contracts/venue_profile.py` → `CLOSED_VENUES`, `bot_closed_reason`, called through `bots/application/services/venue/closed_venue.py` → `closed_venue_refusal` by create, change-venue, start, host start, resume and resume-for-host. There is no "offered flag" in the code; the task's mention of one in `039J` is a plan, not a fact.
- The host's shutdown policy is the port `operations/contracts/i_shutdown_policy.py` → `IShutdownPolicy` (`apply(ledger, give_up)`), with `LeaveOrdersResting` and `StopBotsFirst`; the host picks one from `--on-stop` (`operations/cli/run_command.py`).
- The start already refuses a foreign position on the symbol (`OwnerBudgetRefusal.FOREIGN_POSITION`, `bots/application/kinds/grid/grid_housekeeping.py` → `refusal_text`) and a symbol has one owner (`claim_symbol`).

**What the official documentation says** (🌐, read 2026-10-16; base `https://developers.binance.com/docs/derivatives/usds-margined-futures/`):
- `trade/rest-api/New-Algo-Order` — `POST /fapi/v1/algoOrder`, weight 1 on the 10-second and 1-minute *order* limits and 0 on the IP limit. `algoType` is `CONDITIONAL` only. `closePosition` ("Close-All") is used with `STOP_MARKET` or `TAKE_PROFIT_MARKET`; "Cannot be used with `quantity`", "Cannot be used with `reduceOnly`"; when triggered it closes all of the current long position (for SELL) or short position (for BUY); in Hedge Mode a BUY cannot close LONG nor a SELL close SHORT. `workingType` is `MARK_PRICE` or `CONTRACT_PRICE`, default `CONTRACT_PRICE`. `priceProtect` (default false) limits the trigger when mark and contract price diverge beyond the symbol's `triggerProtect` (from `exchangeInfo`). `clientAlgoId` matches `^[\.A-Z\:/a-z0-9_-]{1,36}$` and is "a unique id among open orders". `selfTradePreventionMode` defaults to `NONE` for algo orders.
- `trade/rest-api/Query-Algo-Order` — `GET /fapi/v1/algoOrder`, weight 1, by `algoId` or `clientAlgoId` (one is required); the answer carries `algoStatus`, `actualOrderId` (empty until triggered), `closePosition`, `workingType`, `triggerPrice`. A `CANCELED` or `EXPIRED` order with no fill is no longer found 3 days after creation; none is found after 90 days.
- `Cancel-Algo-Order` (as listed with the Query page) — `DELETE /fapi/v1/algoOrder`, weight 1, by `algoId` or `clientAlgoId`.
- `user-data-streams/Event-Algo-Order-Update` (read through the site's search summary; the page text itself did not render) — the stream's `ALGO_UPDATE` carries `caid`, `aid`, `ai` (the regular order a trigger placed), `X` (status: `NEW`, `CANCELED`, `TRIGGERING`, `TRIGGERED`, `FINISHED`, `REJECTED`, `EXPIRED`), `wt` (working type), `cp` (close-all), `R`, `rm` (failure reason). `EXPIRED` is "canceled by the system", the example given being a user who closes all positions on the symbol while a `GTE_GTC` conditional order is open.
- `trade/rest-api/Auto-Cancel-All-Open-Orders` — `POST /fapi/v1/countdownCancelAll`; `countdownTime` in milliseconds, `0` disables; "should be called repeatedly as heartbeats". The page does **not** say whether conditional orders are covered.

**What the documentation did not say** (⚠️; the three pages that matter did not render, or are silent): (1) whether a `closePosition` order is **rejected when there is no position** at placement; (2) whether it **survives a position passing through zero** (the `EXPIRED` example suggests it may not); (3) whether a triggered `closePosition` order **with no position on its side** (a BUY stop while the bot is long) does nothing or opens a position; (4) the *exact* error when the trigger is already on the wrong side of the market ("Order would immediately trigger", `-2021`, is documented for the trailing stop only); (5) the order of `ORDER_TRADE_UPDATE` and `ACCOUNT_UPDATE` for one trade; (6) whether `countdownCancelAll` cancels conditional orders; (7) the current-open-algo-orders page (`Current-All-Algo-Open-Orders`) did not render, so its parameters are taken from the repository's use of it (`futures_get_open_algo_orders`) and not re-read. Each is a testnet check in §6; none is guessed here.

| D2 | **One mapping, extended; no parallel one.** `Order` gains `close_position: bool = False` and `working_type: WorkingType = LAST_PRICE`; `map_order_to_futures_algo_params` sends `closePosition=true` and **no `quantity` and no `reduceOnly`** when `close_position` is set, and `workingType` from the order. The parser and the read mapping learn `cp`/`closePosition`/`wt`. | Proposed | 🤖 | Additive in the contract (a defaulted field); the desk's stops are unchanged and a test names that. `quantity` for a close-all order is a request the mapper refuses, so the rejected-order pitfall of the task is closed in one place |
| D3 | The trigger is on **mark price** (`workingType=MARK_PRICE`) and `priceProtect=false`. | Proposed | 🤖 | Liquidation is judged on mark (design 039 §7, R1), so a stop that fires on a last-price wick the mark never reached costs a position for nothing, and one that waits on a last price while mark is already at liquidation is too late. `priceProtect=true` could *withhold* the trigger in a spike, which is the one moment a protective order must fire; the cost is a stop that can fire on a mark/last divergence, accepted for a protective order |
| D4 | **Two kinds, one life cycle:** a *stop loss* (`STOP_MARKET`) and, when the plan has a take profit, a *take profit* (`TAKE_PROFIT_MARKET`), each `closePosition`. A bot's protective orders are told from its ladder by **type and `closePosition`, never by a new id shape**: a ladder never holds a conditional order, so `tag_of(id) == bot tag` **and** `close_position` identifies them, and `client_order_id.py` does not change. The exclusion is **one seam, not a list of call sites** (review F2 of #544): `BotOrderGateway.tagged_open_orders` (`bots/application/services/orders/bot_order_gateway.py`), which every ladder read goes through (`GridStopSequence._confirm`, `GridHousekeeping`, `GridReconciler`, `GridRecoveryReader`, and the `own` count of `futures_facts_reader.py`), returns **ladder orders only**, and a separate `protective_orders()` returns the stops. A guard test fails when any ladder read sees an order with `close_position`. When one of the two is `TRIGGERED` or `FINISHED` **by its algo status, read by `clientAlgoId`** (never by attributing the fill, which depends on the in-memory `AlgoOrderLinks` and on the event order, T5), the other is cancelled in the same step (a closed position's second stop is the orphan the task warns about) | Proposed | 🤖 | No new id pattern means no change to a rule every bot's reconcile depends on (`tag_of`); the cost is one extra predicate in the places that list orders. A bot with no stop-loss price (O3 allows it at leverage 1) has **no stop**, is *unprotected*, and halts by closing |
| D5 | **Persist first, idempotent send.** The stop's `clientAlgoId` is generated and the record `INTENDED` is saved in the bot's runtime **before** the request is sent; `STANDING` is saved on the answer. A crash or a timeout between the two is resolved by `GET algoOrder?clientAlgoId=`: found → `STANDING`; a **not found** answer (its code is ⚠️ T-check; the bot treats the missing order by the answer's class, not by a number the page does not document) → send again with the same id. The page says the id is "unique among open orders", so the id is free again once the order is cancelled, expired or triggered; the resend happens only after a not-found read, so it cannot create a second open order | Proposed | 🤖 | The "unsaved and parked" lesson (`grid_task_guard.py`) holds for the stop; a stop is never an orphan of a crash. The runtime codec (`bots/application/kinds/grid/grid_runtime_codec.py`) gains an optional field: a state file without it reads as "no stop", which is the truth for every bot saved before |
| D6 | **Cancel only when the bot's own exposure is confirmed closed, and say what is left.** A `closePosition` order closes the whole symbol, so it is cancelled only when the bot's derived position is zero **and** the exchange's `get_positions(symbol)` reads one of: zero; a remainder below one market step (*dust*, which the closer already leaves and a stop must not stand for); or the quantity the foreign-position rule already names as not the bot's (R2). Any other reading (a failed read, a close that failed, a position the derivation cannot explain) **keeps the stop** and the stop stays under custody (D13). The cancel is one more step of the stop sequence after `PositionExposureCloser.close` returns `None`, not a decorator that can be skipped; a cancel that answers "unknown order" (`-2011`) or finds the order `EXPIRED`/`CANCELED` counts as done | Proposed | 🤖 | A close that fails leaves the stop standing; "cancel first" is the test that must go red. The same read-before-act applies to a reconcile that finds a stop with no position (D7) |
| D7 | **The restart rules** (one table, §3.2): a position with a matching stop adopts it; with none, the bot re-places it, and if the stop cannot be placed (refused, or the trigger is already beyond the market) it **closes the position reduce-only** and says so; a stop with no position is cancelled once a fresh read confirms flat; a stop that triggered while the app was down returns the bot `STOPPED` flat with the reason `protective_stop_triggered`. A stop found that does not equal the plan's is **replaced** (new one stands, then the old one is cancelled), never cancelled first | Proposed | 🤖 | The bot is never bare at any step of a restart, and never leaves a stop that could open a position |
| D8 | **A stop that vanishes while a position stands is re-placed** (once per detection, then closed on failure), whatever the cause: the exchange's `EXPIRED` (⚠️ T2), a cancel by the owner on the exchange's web page. The desk's Emergency Stop is not a cause (D11 never removes a stop while a position stands); a switched-off session makes the re-arm refuse and the position is reported unprotected. The bot detects it from `ALGO_UPDATE` and from the reconcile's open-order read; **the re-arm is guarded on a position standing** (an `EXPIRED` because the position went through zero re-arms at the next fill that makes it non-zero, not at once) | Proposed | 🤖 | The design does not depend on how T2 turns out: if the exchange keeps the stop through zero it is simply never missing; if it expires it, the bot re-arms after the next opening fill. The cost is one more order per re-arm and a `PROTECTIVE_STOP_MISSING` event the alert module turns into `STOP_FAILED` |
| D9 | **Neutral places both a SELL and a BUY stop-loss** (one-way mode, `closePosition`): the SELL protects a net long, the BUY a net short. Until the testnet check T3 is recorded (a triggered close-all on the side with no position does nothing and does not open one), a Neutral Futures bot is **unprotected** in the sense of D10 and halts by closing. Long and Short place one stop each | Proposed | 🤖 | Neutral needs T3 on every run (its BUY stop stands beside a long). **Long and Short need T3 too, for the windows in which their one stop stands with no position on its side**: between the close's fill and the cancel, whenever a cancel or a read fails (D6), under custody (D13), and when a take profit and a stop loss both stand and one fires before the sibling's cancel (D4). A halt that cannot send an order (switch-off, rejected key, rate limit) leaves the position and the words say whether a stop stands | Proposed | 🤖 | Fails closed: an unreadable exchange is "not standing" |
| D11 | **The desk's Emergency Stop runs three steps (C3):** (1) cancel the *regular* orders, so the ladder's LIMIT orders cannot refill during the close (what today's cancel-first is for); (2) close the positions; (3) cancel the conditional orders of each symbol confirmed flat by D6's rule. `EmergencyStopCommandHandler` cancels everything first today. Needs no owner question: the stop is cancelled once flat, the same rule as D6 |

## 3. The design in two tables

### 3.1 One protective order's life (each protective order; the machine is per order)

| State | Meaning | Left by |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `ABSENT` | no record | `intend` **only while the account position on the symbol is non-zero** and a stop-loss price exists: at the first fill that makes it non-zero (the opening fill, or for a kind that opens flat, the first ladder fill), and again after an `EXPIRED` at the next such fill |
| `INTENDED` | id and trigger price saved, request not (known to be) accepted | `accepted` → `STANDING`; `rejected` → `ABSENT`; if a position stands, close it (D7), if none stands, nothing to close and the next `intend` waits for a position; `lost_answer` → read by `clientAlgoId` |
| `STANDING` | `NEW` on the exchange; it also stands **under custody** (D13) while its bot is `STOPPED` or `HALTED` | `triggered` (decided from the algo status by `clientAlgoId`) → `TRIGGERED`; `exposure_closed` (D6) → `CANCELLING`; `expired` or `missing_with_position` → `ABSENT`, then `intend` again when a position stands (D8) |
| `CANCELLING` | cancel sent after a confirmed flat | `cancelled` or `unknown_order` → `ENDED` |
| `TRIGGERED` | the exchange closed the position | `settled` (flat confirmed, sibling cancelled) → `ENDED` |
| `ENDED` | terminal; the record is dropped on the next save | — |

The code will declare this as a transition table in `bots/domain/grid/` (`code/quality.md` §3); `state-machines.md` renders it from there (generated), as it does for the other two machines. Until then the table above is the design.

### 3.2 What a reconcile or a custody check does with (position, bot's position, stop)

| Exchange position on the symbol | The bot's derived position | Protective order on the exchange | The record | Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ≠ 0 | ≠ 0 | standing, equals the plan | any | adopt; save `STANDING` |
| ≠ 0 | ≠ 0 | standing, differs from the plan | any | place the plan's stop, then cancel the old (never the reverse) |
| ≠ 0 | ≠ 0 | none | `STANDING`/`INTENDED` | re-place; on failure close the position and say why |
| ≠ 0 | ≠ 0 | none | none (old state, or the app died before D5) | place; on failure close |
| ≠ 0 | 0 and the exchange's quantity is dust or the foreign quantity (D6) | standing | any | cancel (the bot's exposure is closed); say what is left and that it is unprotected |
| ≠ 0 | 0 and the exchange's quantity is neither | standing | any | keep; report `exposure_unexplained`; custody holds it (D13) |
| 0 | any | standing | any | orphan: confirm flat with a second read, then cancel |
| 0 | any | `TRIGGERED`/`FINISHED` | `STANDING` | the bot returns flat: cancel its ladder (without any stop), `STOPPED`, reason `protective_stop_triggered` |
| 0 | any | none | any | nothing; drop the record |
| read failed | — | — | — | do nothing, stay `RECOVERING` (or in custody), say why |

## 4. Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Why it lost |
| :--- | :--- |
| Wait for `EPIC-026K` | It has no code and a different shape (per-entry, strategy-owned); the owner decided to build now (D1) |
| A `quantity` + `reduceOnly` stop sized to the bot's own position | Right for "the bot closes only its own" (owner, 2026-10-13), but the quantity goes stale at every fill: a larger position is under-protected, a smaller one risks a rejected trigger; keeping it equal means a cancel/replace per fill, which is the order-rate cost and the failure window this task removes. `closePosition` plus the existing foreign-position refusal (start) keeps the stop simple; the cost (§5, R2) is named |
| A new id shape for stops (`SEW-{tag}-s…`) | Changes the one pattern every reconcile uses to attribute orders; type and `closePosition` already tell a stop from a ladder order |
| `countdownCancelAll` as a dead man | Cancels orders when the app is gone, the opposite of a protective order; the page does not say it spares conditional orders (⚠️) |
| Trigger on last price (the desk's choice) | The desk judges its own stop on last price (`futures_algo_order_mapper.py` → `_WORKING_TYPE_LAST_PRICE`); the bot's job is liquidation distance, judged on mark |

## 5. Risks this design carries (named, not hidden)

| # | Risk | Mitigation, or why accepted |
| :-- | :--- | :--- |
| R1 | A conditional stop that fires after the bot already closed opens the opposite position (026K's warning) | D6 cancel-after-flat; D7 orphan rule; T3 before Neutral |
| R2 | `closePosition` closes the **whole symbol position**, including one the owner opened by hand after the start | The start refuses a foreign position and the symbol has one owner; a foreign fill already halts the bot. Not narrowed by this design; the stop's words say "closes the whole position on <symbol>" |
| R3 | The mark-price trigger may fire on a mark/last divergence | Accepted (D3) |
| R4 | The desk's cancel-all (Emergency Stop, `cancel_all_orders`) and any manual cancel remove the stop | D11 orders the Emergency Stop; D8 re-places after a manual cancel; `cancel_all_orders` is otherwise a deliberate act |
| R5 | A New Algo Order counts on the 10-second and 1-minute order-rate limits like any order (weight 1 each, 🌐) | A bot places one or two per position and re-arms rarely; the rate-limit pause (`grid_rate_limit_pause.py`) applies |
| R6 | Binance docs partly unread (§1 ⚠️) | T1–T5 are owner testnet runs; D8 and D9 are written so that either outcome of T2/T3 is safe in the sense that no step is *bare*; **T3 is a precondition for opening mainnet in every direction** (D9), because every direction's stop stands for a short while with no position on its side |

## 8. What C2 built, and where it departs from the text above
This section is written by the C2 pull request (point in time, `master-warrior` + the C2 branch). The rows above are the decision; these are the places the build, having met the code, differs or learned something. Each is a recorded departure, not a silent one.

| # | Departure or finding | Why |
| :-- | :--- | :--- |
| A1 | **D13 (custody) moves from C2 to C3.** C2 leaves no path on which a stop outlives its bot: a Futures Stop with *keep* is still refused (`keep_on_stop_closed_reason`), and a halt still closes. Custody has a purpose only once C3 lets a halt leave the position. | A custody check with nothing to guard is code that cannot be exercised; C3 is where the first stop is left standing |
| A2 | **D4's "triggered" is read from the order by its client id, once the position says there is something to decide.** A standing record on no open list with a position standing is `MISSING_WITH_POSITION` (D8). With the account flat the bot asks the exchange for the order itself (`IAccountActivity.find_order` → the trading client's by-id read, regular order else algo order, no cache): `TRIGGERED`/`FILLED` is a stop that ran; `EXPIRED`/`CANCELED` is the exchange ending it without a fill (T2) and the record is dropped; an unreadable answer counts as ran (the bot ends flat). | The history reader caches 15 s (`HISTORY_CACHE_TTL`) and does not attribute a triggered algo order's fill to the bot's tag (`AlgoOrderLinks` is in memory and needs the `ALGO_UPDATE` first, T5). The first build of C2 decided from the position alone; review of #545 showed that stops a healthy grid when T2's answer is "expire", so the exact read was added |
| A3 | **D7's "replace" is place-then-cancel-untracked.** A stop that differs from the plan is placed (it stands), then a stray tagged stop the record does not know is cancelled by `_cancel_untracked`. The bot is never bare. | One rule serves a stray stop from a crash and a changed plan |
| A4 | **D9: the Neutral upper stop stands at the take-profit price, not the stop-loss price.** A BUY stop at the long side's price is "on the wrong side" for the mark. Neutral's second side stays behind `NEUTRAL_BOTH_SIDES_VERIFIED = False` (T3), so a Neutral bot is unprotected and closes, as D9 says. | The two sides of a Neutral range are its two exits |
| A5 | **O3: the take profit stays in-app in C2.** Only the stop loss is an exchange order. | The take profit never opens a position by being late; the lifecycle is per order, so adding it later is a record, not a redesign |
| A6 | **A tagged order needs a registered owner budget (`OWNER_BUDGET_MISSING`).** In `RECOVERING` the budget is not registered until the reconcile runs, so the supervision there is read-only; a second look follows the reconcile. | Found by the restart journey |
| A7 | **The two protections can race at one price.** A stop loss in the app and the exchange stop stand at the same level; either may fire first, and the reason is `protective_stop_triggered` when the exchange's did. The bot ends flat and `STOPPED` either way. | Both are intended; the app's check is the one that works with the app down being the exchange's |
| A8 | **Known gaps, accepted for C2.** (1) The closing fill of a triggered stop is not attributed to the bot in history, so its derived position stays stale; the closer is correct because it reads the held amount. (2) With T5's unfavourable event order a ladder re-place can fail (ERROR) before the stop is noticed; the final state is safe. | Both rest on T5 (the owner's) |
| A9 | **A recorded stop that no longer covers the position is released** once the covering one stands (a NEUTRAL grid whose position changed sign while T3 is unrecorded). | Found in review of #545: `_cancel_untracked` skipped every recorded stop, so the long side's SELL stop stood beside a short |
| A10 | **A record left `CANCELLING` by a cancel that did not finish is settled without ever taking a stop down first** (decision D7: place, then cancel). The order gone: the record ends and the position that stands gets a new stop. The order still open over a position on its side again: the same order goes on standing (`exposure_reopened`), so no step leaves the position bare. The order open and covering nothing: the cancel is finished. | Review of #545, round 3: finishing the cancel and then placing a new stop left a bare window |
| A11 | **Known and accepted: a cancel the exchange keeps refusing is retried on each look.** Failed cancels leave the record `CANCELLING` and the order standing; each look settles it by A10 (a position back on its side keeps the same order, nothing resent). No halt comes from this path since A10; a stop that stands over a flat account for as long as the refusals last is the price, and every retry is logged. | Review of #545, round 4: this row described a duplicate-id halt that A10 removed |
