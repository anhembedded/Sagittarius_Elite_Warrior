The Grid backtest reads **Spot** candles and Spot filters (`application/queries/run_grid_backtest/handler.py:70,86,97`) and replays them through `domain/grid/grid_simulator.py` (176 lines) with `grid_fill_rule.py`, `grid_replay.py`, `fine_klines.py`. ## 3. Design
**Model or replay (condition from `039B`).** `grid_position_model.py` is a pure model of a position grid; `GridReplay` replays the real Spot executor. The Futures backtest must either replay the Futures executor (`039H`) the way `GridReplay` does, or have a test that runs the same fills through the executor and this model and compares positions, so the two cannot drift apart unnoticed.

Extend the one simulator with *ports for what differs* (funding schedule, liquidation rule) injected by the profile, the same way costs and risk are; not a Futures copy of the simulator. The simulator stays pure domain (no I/O): funding and candles are passed in.

## Pitfalls
- A candle can contain both the liquidating wick and the recovery: the replay walks a step **leg by leg** and, on each, whatever the price reaches first happens first (liquidation, exit, fill), so the wick is tested before the recovery; the 1-second klines order the legs exactly as the Spot replay's (`steps_for`).
- Do not reuse `paper_exchange.py` as the engine: not reused; its formula is only compared (below).

## Implementation notes
**What shipped** (five commits): (1) `refactor(market_data)`: the raw kline mapping moved out of `PythonBinanceClient` (it sat at 399 lines); (2) `feat(market_data)`: `IFundingHistory`, `FundingEvent`, `IExchangeClient.get_funding_rate_history` (paged), `MarketDataPorts.funding`, a verified fake and a contract suite; (3) `refactor(bots)`: `ReplayEngine` port and `SpotReplayEngine`; (4) `feat(bots)`: `FuturesReplay` and its liquidation/funding/ladder modules; (5) `feat(bots)`: the handler, query and summary.

**A model, not a replay of the executor — said plainly.** `FuturesReplay` is 039B's position model walked over candles with costs, funding and liquidation. It is not a replay of a Futures executor, because none exists before 039H. **Once 039H's executor exists the backtest must replay it, the way `GridReplay` replays the Spot executor, or be tested against it on the same fills** (position, average entry, realised profit after each fill). The module docstring and every Futures result's notes say so ("Modelled, not replayed"). What is checked today is replay against model (above).

**Decisions taken (recorded; reversible):**
1. **Funding history is fetched at run time, not stored.** A symbol settles three times a day; a month is ninety rows. BUG-107's "a backtest never fetches" is about reads nobody asked for; here the user pressed Run and nothing is written. Stated in `IFundingHistory`; a stored implementation is one more class behind the port.
2. **A missing funding history refuses by default.** O9 says "funding and liquidation included" but not what to do when funding cannot be read; the task said "refuse or warn". Refuse never shows a flattered figure; `FundingRequirement.OPTIONAL` is the explicit "run without" the screen (039J) can offer, and the result then says "FUNDING IS NOT INCLUDED" first. **Decided by the coordinator on the owner's behalf (2026-10-13): refuse by default; `OPTIONAL` only when explicitly chosen and offered by 039J. Reversible; the owner is informed and may veto.**
3. **Margin behind a position = its initial margin** `|q| × entry ÷ leverage`, less the funding paid since it was last flat; it is not topped up (a realised profit leaves, a loss is not replaced). At the ladder's worst-case notional (`capital × leverage`, O2) this is exactly `capital_quote`. A liquidation forfeits it whole (loss of the position = its margin; the close at the liquidation price realises part of it, the rest is the exchange's maintenance margin; a gap past the price is capped at the margin).
4. **An exit** (stop loss below the range, take profit above it) closes the position at market whatever its direction, at the taker fee: the parameters name range edges, not a side.
5. **A completed cycle** is a fill that shrinks the position's size. **`grid_profit`** is realised profit net of every commission; funding is apart (039G's rule).
6. **A funding record without a mark price** (older than 2023-11-01) is charged at the price the replay last saw, and the note says how many.
7. Funding timing: the position held at the settlement pays; a settlement at the first candle's time charges nothing (the position opens there); a coarse candle's settlements charge the position it left.

**Not done, by design:** the Futures planner market (filters, fees, daily ATR through `get_planner_market`), the screen that picks a Futures venue, reads the brackets and shows the new rows richly (039J); cross margin; hedge mode; Coin-M.

**Verified / not verified:** the pure replay, the handler on fakes, the funding port's contract and the endpoint's shape are covered by tests that ran. **Nothing was read from Binance** (egress blocked); the funding endpoint's behaviour (paging, `markPrice`'s absence before 2023-11-01) is from its documentation page, and the bracket table used in tests is illustrative, not a recorded answer. Mainnet was not touched anywhere; tests use fakes and hand-built candles.
