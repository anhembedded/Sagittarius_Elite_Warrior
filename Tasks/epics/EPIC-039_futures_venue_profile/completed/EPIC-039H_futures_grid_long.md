## 1. Context and problem
The Grid executor is an actor with one writer per bot (`grid_executor.py`, 286 lines) that delegates to collaborators: `GridStartSequence`, `GridLadderPlacer`, `GridStopper`/`GridStopSequence`, `GridReconciler`, `GridPriceReaction`, `GridHousekeeping`, `GridRecoveryReader`, `GridResumeSequence`, etc. Almost all of that is exposure-agnostic; four things are Spot-shaped and are the **variant points** of this task.

### Facts verified on `master-warrior` `076d339`
- **Start** (`grid_start_sequence.py:95-231`): earlier runs recorded; the opening **MARKET BUY** in slices ≤ the per-order cap (`D21`, `_buy_opening`, `_count_opening`); the bot has trading re-register its budget so inventory is derived from the exchange; the ladder is laid only when that inventory **covers the SELLs**; SELLs sized net of the opening's base-asset fee (`sells_net_of_opening_fee`). RUNNING on `ladder_ready` only once every other level rests.
- **Stop** (`grid_stop_sequence.py:100-237`): `run(base: BaseHandling, price)`: cancel tagged orders (`_cancel_tagged`), `_sell` the base (retry/rate-limit aware), `_confirm` zero open tagged orders → `stop_confirmed`; `grid_stopper.py` + `grid_stop_retry.py` handle STOPPING retries.
- **Halt** parks the ladder: every halt cancels the bot's tagged orders (the `_park` step, named in `grid_start_sequence.py:15` and called from `grid_task_guard.py:123`; find its definition with `grep -rn "def _park\|_park =" src/modules/bots`) and, today, leaves the base (the owner's log of 2026-10-09: six `cancel … -> done`, base left held).
- **Reconcile** (`grid_reconciler.py`, 362 lines — near the 400-line limit): steps 1–7 in its docstring: claim lease, register budget, read tagged open orders, apply fills first, adopt unknown tagged orders, **check the inventory** (`INVENTORY_MISMATCH`, `HOLDING_BELOW_INVENTORY`), persist and transition. `EPIC-035B`: the same steps 2–6 after a user-stream gap (`GridStreamGap`).
- **Price reaction** (`grid_price_reaction.py`): stop loss / take profit fire on the tick's low/high range (`GridTickExtremes`); the stop runs Stop with `SELL_AT_MARKET`.
- The restart rule: RUNNING/PAUSED → RECOVERING; nothing placed or cancelled at boot; the reconcile waits for the venue's trading switch/session (`bot_restore_service.py`, `SPEC-004`).
- File sizes to respect: >400 lines per file and >15 public methods per class force a split (`architecture-rule.md` §5.4).

## Pitfalls
- `grid_reconciler.py` is 362 lines: adding to it breaks the 400-line rule; split first.
- The Spot `_sell` path nets the opening's base fee and waits on balance; the Futures closer must not inherit those assumptions.
- A reduce-only order is *rejected* by Binance when it would not reduce (`-2022`, the fake mirrors it): a ladder out of step with the position produces rejections — they must be classified (`order_outcome.py`: `ORDER_INVALID`) and leave a rung EMPTY, never crash the ladder.
- A halted-then-closed Futures bot has realised a loss: PnL and the alert must say so (`STOP_FAILED` kind in `EPIC-036` covers a close that did not complete).
- Do not hold a position "temporarily" while waiting for the rate limit: the closer's rate-limit wait (`wait_for_rate_limit`) is already modelled for Stop; reuse it, and the position stays under the exchange-side stop meanwhile (039L).

## Implementation notes (written when done)
**Stage 1** (pull request 488, the four seams): a refactor, the Spot suites unchanged. **Stage 2** (this record's second pull request, below): the Futures Long executor runs on the fake. **Stage 3** is not written yet.

### Stage 2 - decisions (all recorded here, none asked; each reversible)
- **A halt on a position venue ends in the Stop, not in HALTED.** O8 says a halt closes the position reduce-only. The close needs the retry, the rate-limit wait and the "confirm flat" the Stop already has (the task's own pitfall: reuse `wait_for_rate_limit`), so `GridParking` takes the ladder off and then runs `GridStopper` with `CLOSE_AT_MARKET`, keeping the halt's reason and adding that any position is closed. The bot ends STOPPED, flat, with the halt's reason, and the lease is released; the user starts a new run. The alternative (stay HALTED and close inside the guard) would duplicate the retry machinery. Spot is untouched (`IExposureCloser.closes_on_halt` is `False`).
- **The close is of the position the exchange reports**, not of the bot's own count (`PositionExposureCloser`): the symbol is leased and a start refuses a standing position, so the exchange's position is the bot's, and when it is not (forced close, ADL, a trade by hand) it is exactly what must not be left bare. A remainder below one market step cannot be sent and is left, said in the log. Flat is confirmed from a fresh read and from nothing else; a read that failed is a wait, never "flat".
- **A Stop that would keep a Futures position is refused in words** (`BotRefusal.EXPOSURE_MUST_CLOSE`, `StopBotCommandHandler`): no exchange-side stop exists before 039L. The headless host's `StopBotForHost` dispatches KEEP, so for a Futures bot it is refused until 039L brings the per-profile policy (O12): nothing is left bare, and the host reports the refusal.
- **Directions: the Futures profile allows LONG only** until 039I (`VenueProfile.directions`), so the existing `DIRECTION_NOT_ALLOWED` sentence refuses SHORT and NEUTRAL in every judge. 039I flips the set.
- **The picker is not changed**: `VenueProfile.offered_in_picker` (Futures `False`) is the one line 039J flips. `bot_profile_of(venue, supported)` and `market_of` take the kind's markets; the Grid passes `GRID_MARKETS`.
- **The ladder is sized by quantity from the venue's limits**: `ExposureLimits.exposure_kind` -> `level_sizing_for` (the plan judge, readiness, preview, the executor and the resume plan all ask it). `GridPlan.uniform_quantity` gives every counter BUY the plan's one quantity (`runtime_from_plan`); `buys_within_capital` keeps a level's quantity until the notional left cannot pay for it (identical for Spot, where the share is the level's own).
- **The owner budget is a port** (`IGridBudgets`, Spot and Futures adapters): a position budget reaches `capital x leverage` and never more than the symbol's bracket allows at that leverage (`allowed_notional`, shared with the plan guard), with the margin as a second ceiling. Brackets are read once per symbol and refreshed at each start (`SymbolBrackets`).
- **Readiness goes through `IVenueFacts` and `judge_venue`** with `FuturesNeeds` (worst-case notional and initial margin of the plan the executor draws); the resume path likewise. `GetExchangeFactsQuery` and the screen's snapshot stay Spot-typed until 039J.
- **The backtest refuses a position venue in words** until 039K (it would draw a profit that ignores funding and liquidation).
- **The plan-time guard runs in the executor's Start** (`FuturesStartChecks`: settings gate, then `judge_plan` over the symbol's brackets, setting and mark read at that moment), not in readiness: a REFUSED verdict is a refused start before any order, in the verdict's words. The daily ATR the stop-distance warning compares with is not read there (the executor has no candles); that one WARNING is judged on the screen's plan.
- **`realized_profit`** is consumed as a cross-check, not summed: the journey asserts the exchange's `rp` of the exits minus the executor's own realised figure is at most the commission paid (039G: a second source for the same number would drift).
- **Resume from HALTED** on a position venue is proposed from the held position (`IExposureCheck.held`, through the bot's `IExposureBook`) with the notional left as the BUY side's budget; it is unit-tested through the plan functions and is reachable only when a close did not complete (a halted Futures bot is otherwise STOPPED).

### Stage 2 - after the review of #490 (2026-10-13)
- **Owner's answer on the close's scope (given in the coordinator session, ~13:01 UTC): a halt, and a Stop with close, closes only the bot's own derived position**, reduce-only, by the quantity the bot built from its own fills. A position the owner opened by hand on the same symbol (or what a forced close left) stays open and is reported in words (`[foreign-position]` in the log, and a sentence on the stop's reason). - **The close does not depend on the cancel.** `GridParking.park` closes in a `finally`: a refused or failing cancel is reported as before ("may still rest") and the stop that closes cancels again with its own retries. - **Futures mainnet is closed on every path** (`CLOSED_VENUES`, `bot_closed_reason`, `closed_venue_refusal`): create, change venue, start (also with `real_money_confirmed`), the host's `--start-planned --confirm-venue futures_mainnet`, resume and the host's `--resume-recovering` each refuse in words before real money is asked about; the readiness's Run step says it too. - **A halt that cannot close says so** (`SWITCH_OFF`, `KEY_REJECTED`, `RATE_LIMITED` send no cancel and no close): on a position venue the halt's words end "any position stays open on the exchange: this bot cannot close it until trading is back". ### Stage 3 - what it decided and found
- **The stop loss and take profit follow the mark, read per beat, not streamed.** No `@markPrice` stream exists in market data; the price watch's 5 s beat reads `premiumIndex`.
