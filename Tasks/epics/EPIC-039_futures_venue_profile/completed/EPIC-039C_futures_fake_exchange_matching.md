## 1. Context and problem
`tests/sanity/fake_exchange/order_book_state.py` says of Futures: "Still not a matching engine: a resting order never fills." A MARKET order fills at once against a fixed book and moves the position and wallet; a reduce-only order that would not reduce is refused with `-2022`; conditional orders live in `futures_algo_orders.py` and trigger on `move_price`. Spot has a matcher (`spot_order_book.py`: `rest`, `take`, `trade_at`). A Futures Grid is a ladder of resting LIMIT orders, so without a matcher its executor can only be tested by mocks — the shape that let `BUG-013` "pass" twice (`fix-bug-rule.md` §4).

## 2. Acceptance criteria
- [x] A resting Futures LIMIT order **fills when the fake's mark/last price crosses it** (BUY when price ≤ limit, SELL when price ≥ limit), partially if the test asks, updating position (signed), average entry, wallet balance, margin and the `positionRisk`/`account`/`userTrades`/`allOrders` the routes report. `move_price(symbol, price)` (existing for algo orders) drives it.
  - **Evidence:** `OrderBookState.move_price(symbol, price, *, fill_at_most=None)` moves the mark, matches (`futures_matching.crosses`), then triggers algo orders, then checks liquidation. Fills are at the order's own limit price, maker fee 0.02 % (what `commissionRate` answers). `test_a_resting_buy_fills_when_price_falls_to_it`, `..._sell_fills_when_price_rises_to_it`, `test_a_fill_is_at_the_limit_price_never_better`, `test_a_limit_placed_already_marketable_rests_until_the_next_move` (the Spot rule, and what keeps `test_a_triggered_stop_places_its_limit_order_and_leaves_history` green), `test_a_partial_fill_leaves_the_rest_resting`, `test_a_fill_pays_the_maker_fee_and_every_report_agrees` (`account`, `userTrades`, `income`, `positionRisk`, `allOrders`). Over HTTP through the real adapters: `test_a_futures_ladder_fills_on_the_fake_exchange.py`.
- [x] One-way semantics: a fill that reduces a position realises PnL; one that crosses zero flips it; a **reduce-only** order that would increase is refused `-2022` (exists) and one larger than the position is capped (exists) — both still hold with resting orders (a resting reduce-only order is cancelled by the fake when the position can no longer be reduced: **to verify** Binance's behaviour first and record the page; implement what the page says).
  - **Evidence:** `test_a_fill_that_crosses_zero_flips_the_position_and_realises_pnl`. **Page read** (`llms-full.txt`, see notes): error `-2022` "the new reduce-only order conflicts with existing open orders", and the order-update page's expiry reason `4` "canceled, as it's knocked out by another higher priority RO (market) order or reversed positions would be opened". Implemented: a reduce-only LIMIT that cannot reduce, or whose quantity with the reduce-only orders already resting exceeds the position, is refused `-2022` (`test_a_reduce_only_limit_with_nothing_to_reduce_is_refused`, `..._on_the_wrong_side_...`, `test_resting_reduce_only_orders_may_not_add_up_to_more_than_the_position`); a resting one that can no longer reduce expires, status `EXPIRED`, `ORDER_TRADE_UPDATE` `x=EXPIRED, er=4` (`test_closing_the_position_expires_a_resting_reduce_only_order`); the market rules are unchanged (`test_a_reduce_only_market_order_keeps_its_old_rules`). **Not on the pages, chosen:** a resting reduce-only order larger than what is left fills what is left and then expires (`test_a_resting_reduce_only_larger_than_what_is_left_fills_what_is_left_and_expires`).
- [x] **Funding:** a test can set a funding rate and advance the fake's clock across a funding time; the position is charged/credited accordingly in the wallet and a user-stream `ACCOUNT_UPDATE` (if a stream exists) or the account read shows it. Field names **to verify** against the official docs (RESEARCH §8).
  - **Evidence:** `set_funding_rate`, `advance_clock(ms)` (`futures_funding.py`), `test_fake_futures_funding.py` (9 tests: long pays and short is paid on a positive rate, a negative rate reverses it, every settlement crossed is charged once, charged at the mark when it settles, a flat account pays nothing, `income` carries a `FUNDING_FEE` row, `premiumIndex` shows the rate and the next time). **Page read:** `ACCOUNT_UPDATE` reason `m = FUNDING_FEE`; a crossed position pushes the balance `B` and no `P`, an isolated one also pushes its `P`; `bc` is the balance change except PnL and commission; the 2026-08-07 changelog adds `a.S` (symbol) for this reason (`test_a_crossed_position_gets_a_balance_only_account_update`, `test_an_isolated_position_gets_its_own_row_in_the_account_update`). **Not on the pages, chosen:** an 8-hour grid, first settlement 8 h after the fake's start, at the mark.
- [x] **Liquidation:** when the mark price crosses the position's liquidation price (the fake computes it with the same formula as `trading/contracts/liquidation_estimate.py`, isolated margin), the fake closes the position, zeroes its margin, cancels the symbol's orders and records the forced fill so the bot can see "a fill I did not order". Identification of a forced close **to verify** (RESEARCH §8).
  - **Evidence:** `futures_liquidation.py` re-implements the formula on purpose; `test_the_fakes_liquidation_price_is_the_production_estimate` compares it with `estimated_liquidation_price`; `positionRisk` now reports `liquidationPrice`, `isolatedMargin`, `maintMargin`. `test_fake_futures_liquidation.py` (9 tests): a long liquidates at or below, a short at or above; the position closes, the symbol's resting orders expire (`er=5`), the whole isolated margin is lost, nothing happens short of the cross. **Page read:** a liquidation order has client order id `autoclose-...` (an ADL close `adl_autoclose`), order type `LIQUIDATION`, execution type `CALCULATED` with status `NEW`; the fake emits `CALCULATED/NEW` then `TRADE/FILLED` for `autoclose-<n>`, records it in `allOrders` (type `LIQUIDATION`) and `userTrades`, and pushes an `ACCOUNT_UPDATE` with the position flat. In the composed app: `test_a_liquidation_reaches_the_app_as_a_fill_it_did_not_order`. **Not on the pages, chosen (worst case):** the forced fill is at the liquidation price, the whole isolated margin is lost, no insurance fund, clearance-fee schedule or ADL; under cross the loss is the realised PnL.
- [x] The fake can be asked for **faults** in the spirit of `EPIC-037E` (a rejected order, a delayed fill, a duplicated report) without changing the default behaviour of existing Spot tests.

  - **Evidence:** `futures` in `tests/integration/modules/bots/conftest.py` (the `BINANCE_FUTURES_TESTNET_*` pair, `Client.FUTURES_TESTNET_URL` and the Spot testnet URL patched, python-binance's loop bindings owned); `futures_fake_exchange.booted_futures()` boots `create_app()` on `FUTURES_TESTNET`; `test_a_futures_ladder_fills_on_the_fake_exchange.py` (3 journeys through the real order client). `exchange` and `booted` are unchanged apart from `app_config` gaining a defaulted `venue` argument. ## 3. Design
Mirror `spot_order_book.py` (same `rest/take/trade_at` verbs) so the two fakes read alike; keep the Futures-only parts (position, funding, liquidation) in `futures_account_state.py`; no file past 400 lines (`architecture-rule.md` §5.4) — split `futures_matching.py` and `futures_funding.py`. Demand over comfort: the fake must be able to be *unfair* (late, duplicated, rejected) because the bugs this app has had were about exactly that.

## Implementation notes (written when done)
**Where the Binance facts came from.** The research session could not read the user-data pages. `https://developers.binance.com/en/docs/llms-full.txt` (8 MB, linked from the site's home page) **is** reachable from here and holds the general pages: the `ORDER_TRADE_UPDATE` example and field list, the execution types and statuses, the liquidation and ADL identification, the expiry reasons, the `ACCOUNT_UPDATE` reasons and the funding rule, the error-code table and the changelog. It holds **no REST endpoint pages** (Change Initial Leverage, Change Margin Type, position information, funding rate, income), so those are still unread: `039E` and `039G` must find another way (the per-endpoint pages on the site).

**Decision: how fills reach the app.** Through the user-data stream's own handler, fed from the fake's queue (`OrderBookState.drain_user_data_events` -> `FuturesUserDataStream._handle_message`), exactly as the Spot world feeds `SpotUserDataStream`; REST reads (`openOrders`, `positionRisk`) show the same fills. The fake still has no websocket (`stream_transport.py`), and no order response carries a resting fill (a LIMIT answers `NEW`), so the stream path is the only one a Futures ladder has.

**Rules taken from the pages** (each pinned by a test named in the criteria): `-2022` text; expiry reasons 4 and 5; `CALCULATED`/`NEW` then `TRADE` for `autoclose-` liquidation orders with type `LIQUIDATION`; `ACCOUNT_UPDATE` `FUNDING_FEE` with `B` only for cross and `B`+`P` for isolated, `bc`, and `S`; `-4046`..`-4048`, `-4161`, `-4067`/`-4068`, `-2019`, `-2027`, `-4164` (not modelled). **Chosen where the pages are silent** (stated at the top of each test file): fills at the limit, maker fee 0.02 % / taker 0.05 %; a marketable limit rests until the next move; resting reduce-only orders may not exceed the position together (read from the `-2022` text); a larger one fills what is left and expires; funding on an 8-hour grid at the mark, first settlement 8 h after the fake's start; liquidation at the liquidation price with the whole isolated margin lost; `er` as a string; open orders reserve no margin; the order of `-2027` before `-2019`.

**Findings the task text lacked** (handed to the tasks they belong to, as criteria):
- **The app counts a duplicated Futures fill report twice** (`039D`): `OrderFilledEvent.trade_id` is `None` for every Futures fill because the parser reads no `t`; a probe with `faults.duplicate_reports` got two events for one fill. Nothing de-duplicates it today.
- **`order_filled_event.py` says Futures reports no per-fill commission**; the page lists `n`/`N` and `rp` (`039G`).
- **`move_price` now also moves the mark**, which it did not: the only prior caller (the algo-order test) is unaffected because the orders a trigger places rest until the next move.
- The fake's `positionRisk` now reports a liquidation price, an isolated margin and a maintenance margin (were `"0"`); `leverage`/`marginType` stay absent (`BUG-114`).
- `OrderBookState` was at 571 lines after the first draft; it was split into `futures_order_checks.py`, `futures_account_settings.py` and `UserDataQueue` to stay under the 400-line ratchet (378).
- Deviation from the task text: the matcher's tests are in `tests/unit/fake_exchange/` (beside the fake's existing tests), not `tests/sanity/`.

**Not modelled** (the next task that needs it adds it): partial fills decided by the book (a test caps them), ADL, `ACCOUNT_CONFIG_UPDATE` and `MARGIN_CALL` events, `GTX`/post-only and `IOC`/`FOK` semantics (`O10` says post-only waits), isolated-margin top-ups, cross-margin liquidation across symbols, the insurance fund, the leverage-bracket maintenance rate for open orders, per-symbol funding schedules, trailing and `closePosition` orders.
