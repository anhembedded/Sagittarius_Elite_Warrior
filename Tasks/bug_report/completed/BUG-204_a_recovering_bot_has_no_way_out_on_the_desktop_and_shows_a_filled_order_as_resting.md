# BUG-204 — A RECOVERING bot has no way out on the desktop, and its Orders and report still show an order that filled as resting

- **Reported:** 2026-10-14 (owner, real-money Spot Mainnet bot `Test_real_cash`, id `ke95g7`, ETHUSDT, capital 35, 6 grids 2348–2667; relayed by the coordinator session)
- **Severity:** 🔴 P1 — real money: after a restart a bot cannot be put back to work from the app, a fill of its own order is not booked and its counter-sell is not laid, and the screen says the filled order still rests
- **Status:** ✅ Fixed (2026-10-14)
- **Board:** A restart left the bot RECOVERING with no Resume cell in the lifecycle table, and the order session (which nothing listens through) opens only by Start, a manual order or the headless flag, so a fill went unseen, Stop only waited for the same session, and the Orders panel and boot report kept showing the filled order as resting; fixed by declaring Resume from RECOVERING (real-money answer, opens the session through `order_session_opening`, then the existing reconcile books the fill once) and by saying on the Orders panel and in the report that a RECOVERING ladder is the saved one.
- **Context:** Bots → restart rule and Resume ([SPEC-014](../../../Docs/SPEC/SPEC-014_run_a_grid_bot.md), [SPEC-004](../../../Docs/SPEC/SPEC-004_enable_and_disable_live_trading.md)) → `src/modules/bots/` → `domain/bot_lifecycle_fsm_matrix.py`, `application/services/` (`bot_runner.py`, `grid_executor.py`, `grid_facts.py`, `grid_reconciler.py`), `application/use_cases/resume_bot/`, `ui/bots_screen/`
- **Environment:** Desktop app on `master-warrior` at `f9bd7ec6`, Spot Mainnet, key can trade; status bar "Spot Mainnet: Connected · 2.20 USDT available · key can trade". Reproduced on the fake exchange only; nothing was placed, tested or simulated against Binance mainnet.

## Reproduction
1. Start a Spot Grid; close the app with the ladder resting; start the app. The bot is RECOVERING and the order session is closed.
2. The price falls through a resting buy and it fills on the exchange.
3. Look at the bot: Orders shows the filled order with Executed 0.0000, Held is unchanged, the plan says "6 saved order(s) rest, 0 filled while the app was closed", Resume and Confirm resume are disabled, Stop is enabled.

Expected: a clear way out of RECOVERING, the fill booked once, its counter-sell laid, the orders and held quantity not claiming the filled order rests. Actual: none of it, every time (the owner's bot; reproduced on the fake exchange).

## Symptom
Owner's observations (transcribed by the coordinator): Binance open orders 5 (buys 2348.00 and 2401.16; sells 2560.67, 2613.84, 2667.00) and free ETH 0.00240050 after the level-2 buy at 2454.33 (qty 0.0023) filled; the bot's Orders panel 6 orders including L2 2454.33 Buy, Executed 0.0000 on every row; Held 0.0067909 unchanged; log only polling queries (`GetBotStatusReportQuery`, `ListBotsQuery`), no fill or order lines; plan panel "Recovering — after the restart: 6 saved order(s) rest, 0 filled while the app was closed, 0 missing, 0 not saved by the bot; nothing is placed until trading is enabled".

The silent log is the evidence for question 1 below: no stream, so no event.

## Root cause
Three links, one cause: **a restart leaves the order session closed and the lifecycle table gave a RECOVERING bot no deliberate action that opens it.**

1. *Why the fill was never seen.* The user-data stream starts in exactly one place, `SessionReadiness._ensure_ready_serialised` (`src/modules/trading/application/session/session_readiness.py:150`, `scope.ports.user_data_stream.start()`), called from `ITradingSession.ensure_ready`. A restart does not open the session (`TradingSessionState` is never persisted, SPEC-004). With no stream no `OrderFilledEvent` is published, so `BotEventRouter.on_fill` (`bot_event_router.py:85`) is never called and `GridFacts._apply_fill` (`grid_facts.py:189`) never runs. The bot's ledger missed nothing it could have heard.
2. *Why the owner could not get out.* `BOT_LIFECYCLE_TRANSITIONS` had no `(RECOVERING, RESUME)` cell (`bot_lifecycle_fsm_matrix.py`), so `availability()` disabled Resume (`bot_action_rules.py:78`) and `ResumeBotCommandHandler` refused it. The session opens only by Start (`GridStartPreconditions.check`), Resume from HALTED (`BotRunner.resume`, `BUG-202`), the headless `run --resume-recovering`, arming a strategy or a manual order (`test_every_order_is_reconciled.py`). A RECOVERING bot could use none of the bot-side ones. Stop is declared from RECOVERING, but `GridStopSequence.run` registers a budget first, which a closed session refuses (`TRADING_SWITCH_OFF`), so the bot sits in STOPPING with `WAITING_FOR_TRADING` (`grid_stop_sequence.py`): it needs the same session. `BUG-202`'s family scan listed every caller of `register_owner_budget` and so found HALTED; it did not ask which lifecycle states wait on the session and have no deliberate opener, so RECOVERING stayed out.
3. *Why the screen claimed the order rests.* A RECOVERING bot's `runtime.open_orders` is the ladder as saved; the only exchange read is the one-shot boot report (`GridRecoveryReader.report`, `grid_recovery_reader.py:62`), stored as the bot's reason text and never refreshed. `BotOrdersPanel` rendered the saved rows as if live, and the report's tail ("until trading is enabled") named no way to enable it.

What the reconcile does with a fill that landed meanwhile (question 3) was already right: once the session opens, `GridFacts._apply_switch` → `GridReconciler.run` → `_apply_missed_fills` → `_catch_up` (`grid_reconciler.py`) reads the order's executed quantity from history, books the difference through `on_fill(..., hold=True)` (the counter order held), records the trade ids in `AppliedFills` (so a late stream delivery does not count it twice), and `release_held` lays the counter order once RUNNING. `test_a_restart_reconciles_a_fill_missed_while_closed_without_a_duplicate` proves it on the fake exchange. The defect was reaching it, not doing it.

The gate was green because every guard on this area pinned who may open the session, none pinned that every state waiting on it has an owner action. Not a case study: the open question (other states waiting on an external event) is answered by the `LEGAL` table in `test_bot_action_rules.py`, which pins RECOVERING to Resume and Stop.

## Fix
- `domain/bot_lifecycle_fsm_matrix.py`: `(RECOVERING, RESUME) → RECOVERING`, a self-loop since the reconcile decides the next state (`reconcile_ok` → PRIOR, `reconcile_mismatch` → HALTED). The Resume button (`is_declared`) and the use case follow the table.
- `application/use_cases/resume_bot/handler.py`: a RECOVERING mainnet resume needs the real-money answer (`REAL_MONEY_NOT_CONFIRMED`), exactly as a HALTED one does after `BUG-202`; the screen's `command_for` already asks the question and sets it.
- `application/services/bot_runner.py`: `resume` opens the venue's order session for RECOVERING as for HALTED, through `open_session_for_resume` → `order_session_opening` (behind the read-only runner), and a session that will not open is refused in its own words, never reported as done.
- `application/services/grid_executor.py`, `grid_facts.py`: the executor's `_run_resume` in RECOVERING calls `GridFacts.reconcile_recovering()`, the body that the switch-on event already ran (extracted, not copied). The opening publishes that event too; whichever reaches the bot first reconciles and the second finds it RUNNING and changes nothing.
- `ui/bots_screen/bot_action_rules.py`: Resume's tooltip for RECOVERING says what it does; `bot_activity_panels.py`: the Orders panel carries a note while the bot is RECOVERING ("saved ladder, not checked with the exchange … an order below may have filled, and the held quantity does not yet count it. Resume checks the exchange and books it."); `domain/grid/recovery_report.py`: the boot report says it is one read at startup and that Resume opens the session and books what filled since; `grid_reconciler.py`: the wait text says "press Resume to retry".

Not done, on purpose: no live refresh of the boot report. A bot that has no session has no stream, and a periodic history read per saved order per beat is a rate-limit cost for a state the owner now leaves with one click.

- `tests/integration/modules/bots/test_a_recovering_bot_resumes_from_the_desktop.py::test_resume_opens_the_session_and_books_a_fill_that_landed_while_recovering` — the composed app over the fake exchange: Start, close, restart with the session closed, the 48,800 buy fills while RECOVERING, `ResumeBotCommand`. **Red before the fix** with `AssertionError: A bot in RECOVERING cannot take the event 'resume'` (the first run, before any change); green after: the session is open, the stream started once, the bot RUNNING, the fill booked (inventory up), its sell at 49,600 laid once, the exchange's open orders equal the bot's ladder price for price with no level twice. - `tests/unit/modules/bots/application/services/test_grid_resume_from_recovering.py` — the executor books the fill once with its counter order; the switch-on event after a Resume changes nothing.
- `tests/unit/modules/bots/application/services/test_bot_runner_resume.py` — a RECOVERING resume opens a closed session and reaches the executor; one whose session will not open is refused and queues nothing.
- `tests/unit/modules/bots/application/test_resume_a_recovering_bot.py` — a mainnet RECOVERING resume without the real-money answer is refused and queues nothing; with it, queued; testnet and PAUSED need none.
- `tests/unit/modules/bots/ui/bots_screen/test_a_recovering_bot_does_not_claim_its_orders_rest.py`, `test_bot_action_rules.py`, `tests/unit/modules/bots/domain/test_bot_lifecycle_fsm_matrix.py` — the Orders note appears for RECOVERING only and goes with it; Resume is live for RECOVERING, Confirm resume is not; Resume sends the real-money answer the user gave; the table declares the new cell.
