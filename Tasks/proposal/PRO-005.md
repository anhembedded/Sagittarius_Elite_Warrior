## 1. Problem and evidence

The application can already send a real order — to Binance USD-M Futures **Testnet** — and the
path it takes is better guarded than most hobby bots: signed requests with a measured clock offset,
quantity and price rounding against the venue's own filters, a pre-flight minimum-notional check,
four session limits, a per-symbol lease, an app-generated client order id on every order, order
truth taken from the user data stream rather than the submit response, reconciliation at enable,
and a three-step Emergency Stop. All of that was measured on 2026-09-20 by reading the tree
(the survey is in the epic README §1); nothing here is inferred from task files.

What is **missing** was measured the same way, and it falls into three groups.

### 1.1 The strategy's edge is unproven

- `EPIC-001B` — running the reference strategy on TradingView and diffing the trade list against
  the app's — has never started
  ([`Tasks/epics/EPIC-001_.../incomplete/EPIC-001B_...md`](../epics/EPIC-001_ema_trend_pullback_tradingview_cross_reference/incomplete/EPIC-001B_run_and_diff_tradingview_vs_app_trade_lists.md)).
- Out-of-sample validation exists as a single split
  (`src/modules/backtesting/domain/out_of_sample_split.py`); rolling walk-forward and Monte Carlo
  are backlog (`BOT-107`, `BOT-107A`, `BOT-107B`).
- The direction fixed in [`Tasks/ROADMAP.md`](../ROADMAP.md) ("Backtest đáng tin trước, giao dịch
  thật gác lại" — *trustworthy backtests first, real trading deferred*) was set in 2026-08 and has not
  been revisited since `EPIC-021` closed. `BOT-008` is still explicitly locked.

### 1.2 The operational half was never needed on Testnet

| Gap | Evidence (checked 2026-09-20) | Why it matters with real money |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Nothing about trading is persisted | `src/modules/trading/application/trading_session_state.py:1-4` says so by design; no order, fill or position journal exists anywhere under `src/modules/trading/` | A crash with an open position leaves no record of what this app did; the next enable refuses the account (`enable_trading/handler.py:119-125`) |
| No retry, no idempotent resend, no explicit `recvWindow` | `-1003`/`-1015` are only *classified* (`binance_error_translator.py:36-39`); `recvWindow` appears in comments only; timeout 30 s (`binance_endpoints.py:31`) | A timed-out submit is indistinguishable from a rejected one; the client order id that would make a resend safe is generated but never used to query |
| Leverage is never sent to the exchange | `futures_change_leverage`/`futures_change_margin_type` occur nowhere in `src/`; `trading.live_leverage` feeds sizing only (`position_sizing_bridge.py:42-61`) | A real fill uses whatever leverage the account happens to hold |
| No protective stop on the exchange | `LiveTradingCoordinator` sends `MARKET` only; `STOP_MARKET` exists on `OrderType` (`order_type.py:19`) but nothing constructs it | A dropped connection leaves a naked position; the strategy's exit needs the app alive |
| No daily-loss or drawdown circuit breaker | The four limits are counts and notional (`config_keys.py:83-92`); none reads PnL | A strategy that is wrong all day is stopped only by a human |
| No out-of-band alerting, no watchdog | `BOT-018` is backlog; a dead user-data stream logs and nothing else (`futures_user_data_stream.py:119`) | The operator learns about a problem when they next open the app |
| Session factory hard-codes Testnet | `futures_session_factory.py:93,113` (`testnet=True`) | Mainnet is unreachable for orders by construction — correct today, and the exact place the change must be reviewed |

### 1.3 Mainnet does not exist as a type

`TradingVenue` has two members (`trading_venue.py:14-15`); its docstring and `EPIC-021`'s ADR §3
say adding `MAINNET` is a separate epic whose review *is* the safety mechanism. No such epic or
proposal existed before this one.

### 1.4 Three use cases the app performs but has not specified

`SPEC-006` (cancel one order), `SPEC-007` (Emergency Stop) and `SPEC-010` (arm and disarm a
strategy) are reserved and 🔵 in [`Docs/SPEC/README.md`](../../Docs/SPEC/README.md) although the
code exists. By the repository's own rule a use case without a "Proven by" table is a plan, not a
capability.

## 2. Intended outcome and scope

**Outcome.** The app trades a small, staged amount of real money on Binance USD-M Futures
mainnet, with one strategy on one symbol, and every one of the seven gaps in §1.2 closed *and
soaked on Testnet first*. Each stage ends at a gate with a written, checkable criterion; no stage
starts before the previous gate is recorded as passed.

**Success criteria.**

1. The three reserved SPECs are ✅ with test paths that exist.
2. A dated report under `Tasks/reports/` shows one candidate strategy passing thresholds the user
   fixed *before* the run.
3. A 14-day unattended Testnet soak ends with a journal-versus-exchange reconciliation of zero
   unexplained differences.
4. `TradingVenue.FUTURES_MAINNET` exists, is reviewed by a different session, and is unreachable
   without a typed acknowledgement and its own credentials.
5. The first real-money stage runs with hard caps smaller than Testnet's and produces a
   live-versus-backtest comparison before any scale-up.

**Constraints.** No Engine change is expected. Nothing here weakens an existing guard: the
two-file allowlist for `OrderSubmissionMode.LIVE` and the AST guard on `TradingVenue` stay.
Dependencies (`requirements.txt`) are not touched without a separate approval.

**Out of scope.** Spot, COIN-M, options, multi-symbol portfolios, parameter optimisation
(`BOT-108`), the backtest simulator's trailing stops (`BOT-105`), report persistence (`BOT-115`).
They do not shorten the path to real money.

## 3. Options and trade-offs

| Option | Benefit | Cost and risk | Evidence or precedent |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Keep the current approach: stay on Testnet, keep improving the simulator | Zero capital at risk; the backtest work in backlog is well specified | The stated goal is never reached; the seven operational gaps stay invisible because Testnet never punishes them | `Tasks/ROADMAP.md` direction block; 54 backlog items, none about mainnet |
| Add `MAINNET` now and trade small | Fastest to a real fill | Every §1.2 gap is met with real money at stake; a crash or a naked position is the first lesson, not a test | `EPIC-021`'s ADR §3 rejected exactly this: "not a configuration flip" |
| **Five gated stages** (this proposal): close the open specs → prove the edge → harden operations on Testnet and soak → open mainnet behind an ADR → stage capital | Each gap is closed where failure is free; the mainnet change is one reviewed enum member plus factory parameter; the user decides thresholds and capital, not the code | Longest calendar path (estimate in `TRACKING.md`); Phase 1 depends on the user's own TradingView run | The pattern `EPIC-021` used successfully: read-only first contact, then dry run, then one live order, each stage a runnable milestone |

## 4. Recommendation

The five gated stages, delivered as one epic (`EPIC-026`) so that the gates are rows in one board
and the Gantt shows the critical path. Stage 1 (edge) and stage 2 (operations) are independent and
run in parallel; stage 3 (mainnet) waits for both; stage 4 (capital) repeats.

| Stage | Gate | What closes it |
| :-: | :--- | :--- |
| 0 | Nothing open behind us | `EPIC-025B`/`C` confirmed by the user on Testnet; `SPEC-006`/`007`/`010` ✅ |
| 1 | The edge is real | A candidate report against thresholds fixed in an ADR before the run; `EPIC-001B` done |
| 2 | Operations survive without a human | Journal, recovery, resilient submission, leverage set, exchange-side stop, circuit breaker, alerting, watchdog — then a 14-day soak reconciled to zero |
| 3 | Mainnet exists and is locked | `FUTURES_MAINNET` behind an ADR, separate credentials, typed acknowledgement, smaller caps, read-only first contact, independent review |
| 4 | Capital is earned, not assumed | Stage sizes decided by the user; a live-versus-backtest report before each increase |

**Unresolved questions that could change the recommendation.**

- The thresholds for gate 1 (profit factor out of sample, maximum drawdown, minimum trade count)
  are a risk-appetite decision, not an engineering one. They are `❓ O1` in the ADR.
- Whether a position the journal recorded as ours should be *adopted* on restart (recommended) or
  always refused (today's behaviour) is `❓ O2`.
- The first mainnet stage size is `❓ O3`.

## 5. Decision needed

The user decides, under `ONBOARDING.md` §7:

1. Whether to accept this direction — it reverses the 2026-08 deferral of real trading, by making
   the deferral's own condition (a proven edge) a gate rather than an open end.
2. The three open questions above.

Acceptance commits the project to `EPIC-026`'s 19 sub-tasks and to not adding `MAINNET` outside
that epic's ADR. It does not commit any capital: stage 4 is a separate, repeated decision.
