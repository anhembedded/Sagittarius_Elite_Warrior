# PRO-015 — Discord tells the owner about every order fill, the profit of every grid cycle, and every exit of a bot

**Date:** 2026-10-18
**Status:** Accepted (2026-10-19), Phase 1 as written
**Source:** The owner, 2026-10-18, running grid bots on a VPS: *"tui muốn Discord báo khớp lệnh, chốt bao nhiêu lời, v.v..."* ("I want Discord to tell me about fills, how much profit was taken, and so on"). Settled in the same conversation: one message per order, no batching (*"báo từ lệnh, ko cần gộp, chứ làm như vậy trước"*), and the heartbeat is not wanted now. Asked to write this proposal and not to build it yet (*"thui ko làm ngay, viết proposal như vậy đi"*).
**Decision:** Accepted by the owner on 2026-10-19, Phase 1 as written: *"không, bây giờ hãy làm cái task thêm thông báo trên Discord đi"* ("no, now do the Discord notification task"). Delivery task: [BOT-189](../backlog/BOT-189_discord_tells_every_order_fill_cycle_profit_and_exit.md).

## 1. Problem and evidence

Today the owner learns that a bot did something by opening the Bots tab or Binance. Discord (`EPIC-036`) already reaches the owner when the owner is away, but it only says that something went **wrong**.

Checked in the repository at `0e072a3` (2026-10-18), by reading and by text search, not by running:

- `src/modules/alerting/application/sources/bot_alert_source.py` publishes `BOT_HALTED`, `BOT_ERROR`, `STOP_FAILED`, `BOT_RECOVERY_STALLED` and `BOT_AUTO_RECOVERED`. The VPS log says the same: `[alert-source] watching N bot(s); an alert on entering HALTED, ERROR and when a stop gives up`.
- `src/modules/alerting/domain/alert_catalog.py` already declares `STOP_LOSS_HIT`, `TAKE_PROFIT_HIT`, `RANGE_EXIT`, `RATE_LIMITED`, `PRICE_FEED_STALE`, `USER_STREAM_DOWN`, `HEARTBEAT` and `DAILY_SUMMARY`. A search of `src/` for `AlertKind.<name>` finds no code that publishes `STOP_LOSS_HIT`, `TAKE_PROFIT_HIT`, `RANGE_EXIT`, `HEARTBEAT` or `DAILY_SUMMARY`. Declared, not fed.
- No kind says "an order filled" or "a cycle earned".
- The facts exist in `bots`: `BotOrderFill` (`src/modules/bots/contracts/bot_order_events.py`, with `realized_profit` on Futures, `EPIC-039G`), and the profit of a completed cycle, `(sell − buy) × quantity − both legs' fees`, booked once per cycle by `book_cycle` (`src/modules/bots/domain/grid/grid_books.py`, described in `grid_reactions.py`). A full fill emits exactly one counter order, so "a full fill" is already a well-defined event.
- The machinery to deliver them exists: the SQLite outbox that survives a restart (24 h expiry), one worker lane per channel with `retry_after` and bounded attempts, dedup, quiet hours, the per-bot `BotAlertMode` (`ON`, `CRITICAL_ONLY`, `OFF`) and the `Loudness` of a route cell (`SILENT` is sent without a sound). The Discord channel is configured by the webhook credential.

Unknown, to be settled by whoever builds it: whether the live fill and cycle facts reach `alerting` only through `bots/contracts` today (the module boundary says alerting listens to `bots/contracts`, so a cycle-completed fact may need a new contract event); and what Discord does to a webhook that receives bursts of messages (its published limit is about 30 requests a minute per webhook; not measured here).

## 2. Intended outcome and scope

**Phase 1, the subject of this proposal:**
1. **One Discord message per fully filled order**: bot name, symbol, side, price, quantity, level. Partial fills do not send (the ladder only reacts to the fill that completes an order). The message carries a stable id so a restart never repeats it.
2. **On the fill that completes a grid cycle**, the same message adds the net profit of that cycle, the cumulative realized profit of the run and the number of cycles.
3. **Exits, sent at once and loud**: a stop loss that ran (price, position closed, realized loss), a take profit reached in the application, the price leaving the range and what the bot did (`on_range_exit` hold or close).

An example line: `Future_ETH_TestNet · ETHUSDT · BUY 0.019 @ 2,393.17 (level 7) · cycle +0.41 USDT · total +12.8 USDT · 31 cycles`.

**Out of scope, on purpose:** batching fills into digests (the owner chose one message per order), the heartbeat (`EPIC-036E`, not wanted now), a near-liquidation alert, the daily summary, any channel other than Discord, and anything that changes an order or a bot's behaviour: the feature only reads and reports.

Success is observable: with a fake channel, a bot run on the fake exchange produces one message per full fill, a cycle message carrying the profit that `book_cycle` booked, and an exit message for each exit; nothing is sent from a read-only copy of the data root; a restart does not resend; a CRITICAL alert is never delayed behind fill messages.

## 3. Options and trade-offs

| Option | Benefit | Cost and risk | Evidence or precedent |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Keep the current approach | Nothing to build | The owner finds out about fills and profit only by looking | Today's alert kinds |
| **A. One message per full fill, plus the exits** (this proposal) | What the owner asked for; reuses the outbox, the worker and the per-bot modes | A busy grid can send many messages; Discord throttles a webhook | `bot_alert_source`, `BotAlertMode`, `Loudness` |
| B. Digest of fills every N minutes | Few messages, no throttling | Not what the owner chose; a fill is reported late | The owner's words of 2026-10-18 |
| C. Per-fill messages only on the Bots tab and the log | No Discord traffic | Does not reach the owner who is away, the point of `EPIC-036` | — |

## 4. Recommendation

Option A, in two cuts of the same change:

1. **Source.** A new alert source in `alerting/application/sources/`, next to `bot_alert_source.py`, fed only through `bots/contracts`. If the cycle profit is not on a contract event yet, `bots` publishes one (the fact belongs to `bots`: it is the context that books the cycle). New `AlertKind` rows in `alert_catalog.py` for an order fill and a cycle (the catalog test fails if a row is missing), and a publisher for the declared `STOP_LOSS_HIT`, `TAKE_PROFIT_HIT` and `RANGE_EXIT`.
2. **Delivery safeguards, because a grid fills often.** Fill messages are INFO and `SILENT` (no sound on the phone); the exits are CRITICAL or WARNING and loud. A bot can be set to `CRITICAL_ONLY` or `OFF` with the mode that exists. If the queue of fill messages grows past a bound, the oldest fill messages are dropped and replaced by one line ("N fill messages skipped"), so a critical alert never waits behind them. The id of the order, with the bot, is the dedup key.

Dependencies and open questions that could change this: the contract event for the cycle (a small change in `bots`); the bound and the wording of the skipped-messages line; whether the owner wants the fill messages for all bots by default or switched on per bot (the existing mode defaults decide it).

## 6. Outcome and follow-up

Accepted 2026-10-19; delivered by [BOT-189](../completed/BOT-189_discord_tells_every_order_fill_cycle_profit_and_exit.md) (PR #572). Built: one silent message per order that filled whole, the cycle's booked profit on the fill that closes a cycle, a loud message for a stop loss, a take profit and a range exit that closes, a WARNING for a range exit that only holds, and a bounded fill queue with one "N fill messages skipped" line.

Two departures from §2.3 as accepted, kept as the author recommended and awaiting the owner's yes (the owner may veto):
1. The exit message goes out when the stop **starts**, so it does not carry the realized loss of the close: [BOT-190](../completed/BOT-190_discord_tells_the_realized_result_when_an_exit_has_closed_the_position.md) adds one INFO on entering STOPPED with the run's realized total.
2. A range exit where the bot only holds is WARNING, which the defaults send silent; §2.3 said loud. A price at the edge is frequent and a loud ping each time trains the owner to ignore it; the owner can route that group loud once the settings persist.

Known limit: nothing sets `BotAlertMode`, so the fill messages cannot be muted per bot: [BOT-191](../completed/BOT-191_the_owner_mutes_fill_messages_per_bot.md).

The owner's later options, each its own proposal: a daily summary, a near-liquidation alert, the heartbeat (`EPIC-036E`).
