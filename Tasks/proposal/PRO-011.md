# PRO-011 — Closing the desktop app says what happens to running bots, then shows its shutdown steps before the window goes

**Date:** 2026-10-14
**Status:** Proposed
**Source:** The owner, 2026-10-15, after a close printed two shutdown WARNINGs (BUG-143): *"mình có cần thiết kế closing process và progress bar không? đóng các kết nối v.v. rồi mới tắt UI"* (do we need a closing process with a progress bar, closing the connections before the UI goes?). Then: *"ghi thành PRO-011 đi, nhiều việc quá rồi"* (write it as PRO-011; there is too much work already).
**Decision:** Pending (owner)

## 1. Problem and evidence
- Closing the main window today ends the app without a word about bots. The owner's real-money bot `ke95g7` was RUNNING when the app closed. On the next start it was RECOVERING and waited for Resume. The screen gave no warning beforehand, and this surprised the owner twice on 2026-10-14 (BUG-204, BUG-206).
- The shutdown already runs in order, as the owner's log of 2026-10-15 00:29 shows: each extension is stopped and disposed, then `AsyncRuntime` stops. None of this is visible after the window closes. A slow step, here the Spot user-data stream that did not cancel within 5 s (BUG-143), shows up only as WARNING lines in a console the owner may not be watching.
- Leaving orders resting on a stop is decided behaviour (decision O1 in `Docs/OPERATIONS/headless_runbook.md` §0). What is missing is telling the owner about it at the moment of closing.

## 2. Intended outcome and scope
1. **Confirm before closing when a bot is live.** If any bot is RUNNING, PAUSED or RECOVERING, closing asks first. The dialog names each such bot with its venue, and states that:
   - its orders stay on the exchange;
   - nothing watches them until the app runs again;
   - on the next start the bot is RECOVERING and needs Resume.

   Mainnet bots are marked. With no live bot, closing does not ask.
2. **Show the shutdown.** After confirmation, the window stays and a modal "Closing…" lists the shutdown steps as they complete: stopping bot workers, closing streams, saving state, stopping the runtime. A step that runs long, or fails, says so on screen.
3. **Bounded.** The whole sequence has a ceiling of about 10 s. Past it, the app records which step did not finish and exits. Closing never hangs.

Out of scope:
- Cancelling orders on close, which would change decision O1.
- The headless host, whose stop is already logged (`DRAINING -> STOPPED`).
- Fixing a slow step itself (BUG-143 does that).

## 3. Options and trade-offs
| Option | Benefit | Cost and risk | Evidence or precedent |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Keep the current approach | No work | The owner learns about RECOVERING only after reopening. A slow shutdown is visible only in the console | BUG-204, BUG-206, BUG-143 on 2026-10-14 |
| Confirmation dialog only | Small; closes the money-safety gap | Shutdown progress stays invisible | Real-money confirmation dialogs already exist (`App.RealMoney`) |
| Confirmation plus a progress modal (recommended) | Closes the safety gap and makes every shutdown step visible | Shutdown progress must be reported from the extension stop order to the UI without blocking the Qt thread, under `async-ui-action-rule.md`; one more modal to test | The ordered extension stop already exists, and the async UI action pattern (`async-ui-action-rule.md`) applies |

## 4. Recommendation
Confirmation plus a progress modal, delivered **after BUG-143 is fixed**. Before that fix, the modal would stop for 5 s on the user-data stream at every close. The confirmation alone could ship first if the owner wants the safety part sooner.

## 5. Decision needed
The owner accepts, rejects or defers this proposal. Acceptance commits the project to one standalone task (`BOT-*`), or two if the confirmation ships first. Each task is UI work with presenter tests and a preview, and changes no order behaviour.
