# PRO-010 — A bot halted by the budget after an order-quantity shortfall names the shortfall as the cause

**Date:** 2026-10-14
**Status:** Proposed
**Source:** The BUG-205 review (PR #507) raised it as an owner question. The owner, 2026-10-14: *"cái này đưa vào pro đi, nhiều việc quá rồi"* (put this in a proposal; there is too much work already).
**Decision:** Pending (owner)

## 1. Problem and evidence
- After BUG-205, the restart reconcile aligns each saved order with the quantity the exchange holds before it applies missed fills (`bots/application/kinds/grid/recovery/grid_order_quantities.py`). A smaller exchange quantity is adopted with a WARNING `[order-quantity]`; a larger one halts the bot with `order_quantity_mismatch`.
- Case found in review: a sell that the exchange held for well below the saved quantity fills whole while the app is closed. The base the exchange did not sell stays in the inventory, so the following counter BUY is refused by the owner budget. The bot halts as `order_refused: owner_budget_exposure`.
- The halt is safe: nothing is placed. But its reason names the budget, not the quantity shortfall that caused it, so the owner reads a budget problem and looks in the wrong place. A one-step shortfall, such as the owner's L6 (0.0022 saved, 0.0021 held), stays RUNNING.
- Evidence: the reviewer's comment on PR #507 and `test_a_restart_settles_an_order_held_for_less_that_filled_whole_while_closed`, whose docstring records the refused counter BUY.

## 2. Intended outcome and scope
When a budget refusal follows a quantity adopted in the same reconcile, the halt reason and the bot's state line name the shortfall: the order, the saved and held quantities, and the leftover base. Example: "halted: L6 was held for 0.0011 less than saved; the leftover 0.0011 ETH exceeds the budget for the next buy".
- Halt behaviour is unchanged.
- No new automatic action, such as selling the leftover.
- Out of scope: what the bot should do with the leftover base.

## 3. Options and trade-offs
| Option | Benefit | Cost and risk | Evidence or precedent |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Keep the current approach | No work | The owner sees a budget halt with no visible link to the shortfall; only the earlier WARNING line in the log connects them | PR #507 review |
| Carry the adoption into the halt reason (recommended) | One message tells the owner what happened; the halt behaviour is untouched | Small: the reconcile already knows what it adopted; the halt reason needs a field and the state line needs wording | Halt reasons as data with screen wording (BUG-206 `restored_paused`) |
| Halt directly with `order_quantity_shortfall` before the counter BUY is tried | Clear, single reason | Stops bots that could have kept running when the budget still allows the BUY; changes real-money behaviour | `order_quantity_mismatch` for the larger case |

## 4. Recommendation
Carry the adoption into the halt reason. It changes only what the owner reads, keeps the fail-safe exactly as reviewed, and follows the BUG-206 pattern of passing the cause to the screen as data. It is a single bug-fix-sized task, with no reviewer needed unless the owner asks (`ONBOARDING.md` §7).

## 5. Decision needed
The owner accepts, rejects or defers this proposal. Acceptance commits the project to one standalone task (`BOT-*`) that changes the halt reason and wording only.
